Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions about our free, private online PDF tools? Here are answers to common questions.

Is PDF Suite completely free to use?

Yes, PDF Suite is 100% free with no hidden paywalls, no monthly subscription fees, no credit card required, and no daily file conversion caps. All tools are open and available for personal, academic, and business use.

Are my confidential files or PDFs uploaded to any server?

No, never. Unlike conventional online PDF converters that send your confidential documents to third-party cloud servers, PDF Suite performs 100% of processing locally inside your web browser. Your sensitive legal documents, medical records, financial statements, and personal files never leave your computer.

Can I edit existing text inside an existing PDF document?

Yes! With our flagship Edit PDF Text tool, you can click directly on existing text lines within your PDF document to modify or replace the text in real-time. You can also add new text boxes, adjust font sizes and colors, apply bold formatting, align text, whiteout areas, and add digital signatures.

How do I convert PDF to Word (.docx) or Excel (.xlsx) for free?

Simply select the PDF to Word or PDF to Excel card above, choose your PDF file, and click convert. PDF Suite parses the vector layout and text streams, reconstructs tables and paragraphs, and packages a genuine .docx or .xlsx document for immediate download.

How does client-side PDF and image compression work?

PDF Suite optimizes embedded bitmap streams, strips redundant object metadata, and re-encodes raster assets using HTML5 canvas and modern compression algorithms. You can select between Recommended (Good), Extreme, and Less compression based on your file size requirements.

Can I merge multiple PDFs or delete specific pages?

Yes! Use the Merge PDF tool to combine multiple PDF documents into a single consolidated file. Alternatively, use the Split & Delete Pages tool to view thumbnail previews of all pages, click the delete button on any page you wish to remove, select page ranges, or export kept pages as individual files.
